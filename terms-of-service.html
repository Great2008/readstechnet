1. Definitions

1.1 Tutors — Verified teachers who are paid to conduct one-on-one (1v1) learning sessions with learners online.

1.2 Tutorial Centre — Functionally similar to a Tutor, except tutors operate as a group under a single umbrella body (the "Centre"), which manages session requests and payouts on their behalf.

1.3 School — A verified physical or online school registered as a partner on the platform.

1.4 Examination Centre — A physical registration centre responsible for the physical registration of exams on behalf of learners.

1.5 Learner — The normal user of the READS platform, who books sessions with Tutors or Tutorial Centres, and/or registers for exams through an Examination Centre.

1.6 READS — The READS team, being the operator of the platform and the party responsible for verification, review, and enforcement of these terms.

2.2 This 5% service fee excludes transaction (network/gas) fees, which are charged separately.

2.3 The service fee is paid by the sender of the transaction.

3. Tutors

3.1 A session is verified through the completion process set out in 3.2 below: either the Learner marking it complete, or the Learner accepting a Tutor-marked completion. Learner confirmation is itself the verification step — no separate READS check is required before payout, except where a dispute is raised.

3.2 Session Completion & Disputes

  • If a Learner marks a session as completed, the Tutor is paid out immediately.
  • If a Tutor marks a session as complete, the Learner will be asked to accept or reject the completion:
    • If the Learner accepts, payment is released as normal.
    • If the Learner rejects (e.g. because the session was not actually completed), the session is submitted to READS as a dispute.
    • READS will review the dispute and issue a decision resulting in either half payment or no payment to the Tutor.
  • If a party believes there was an error in a dispute decision, that decision may be appealed in line with the Appeals Procedure in Section 10.

4. Tutorial Centres

4.1 Session Requests — When a Learner requests a session, the Tutorial Centre (via its manager) is responsible for accepting the request and routing it to the next available Tutor.

4.2 Responsibility for Conduct — Tutorial Centres bear full responsibility for the misbehaviour of any Tutor under their umbrella, as it pertains to sessions booked through that Centre.

4.3 Payout on Completion & Disputes — The session completion, payout, and dispute resolution process set out in Section 3.2 applies equally to Tutorial Centres, save that any reference to a "Tutor" in that process refers instead to the Tutorial Centre managing the Tutor who conducted the session.

5. Examination Centres

5.1 Registration Status — All exam registrations are marked as pending until the Examination Centre accepts them.

5.2 Physical Registration — Upon acceptance, the Centre sends notice to the Learner indicating when to appear for physical registration. The registration then moves to "Pending Centre Processing" status.

5.3 Completion & Review — The registration remains in "Pending Centre Processing" status until the Centre:

  • Completes the registration, and
  • Submits proof of completion for READS review.

5.4 Final Approval — A registration is only marked complete once READS approves the submitted proof.
