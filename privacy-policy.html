Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how READS ("we," "us," the "READS team") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information belonging to Learners and Partners (Tutors, Tutorial Centres, Schools, and Examination Centres) who use the platform. This Policy forms part of, and should be read alongside, the READS Terms & Conditions.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Identity & Contact Information — Name, email address, phone number, and similar identifying details provided at sign-up.

2.2 School/Class Affiliation — For Learners joining via an institution code: class, session, and admission number.

2.3 Session & Exam History — Records of sessions booked, completed, disputed, or appealed, and exam registrations and results.

2.4 Wallet Address — The wallet address used to send and receive $READS tokens on a supported blockchain network. Depending on the blockchain supported by READS, this may be a Cardano wallet or another compatible blockchain wallet. Transactions recorded on a public blockchain are, by their nature, publicly visible and traceable on that blockchain's ledger, independent of anything READS does. Once recorded on a public blockchain, this information cannot be altered or deleted by READS.

2.5 Minors' Consent Records — Where a Learner is a minor, the parent/guardian consent record required to activate their account.

2.6 Exam Integrity Data — During a CBT exam, front-camera captures and incident logs (tab-switching, copy attempts, timing data) collected solely to support the anti-cheat review process described in the Learner Terms.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above to: operate and maintain the platform; verify identity and eligibility; process payments, payouts, and refunds; review disputes and academic integrity flags; communicate with Learners and Partners (including via email, SMS, or push notification); and comply with legal obligations.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data on the following bases: performance of our contract with you (the Terms & Conditions); compliance with legal obligations; your consent where required (such as parental consent for minors), and, where applicable, your agreement to camera monitoring as a condition of participating in certain monitored CBT examinations; and our legitimate interests in operating a safe and functioning platform.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following categories of third-party service providers to operate the platform:

  • Database hosting: Supabase
  • Application hosting: Vercel
  • Blockchain infrastructure: Blockfrost (for Cardano); this may change if READS moves to a different blockchain, in which case this Policy will be updated accordingly
  • Email, SMS, and push notification providers, for account and platform communications

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own independent purposes.

6. Cookies & Session Data

READS does not currently use third-party analytics or tracking tools. We do use essential session/authentication cookies or equivalent local storage needed to keep you securely logged in and to protect the platform from abuse. If third-party analytics are introduced in future, this Policy will be updated to describe what is used and why.

7. International Data Transfers

Because we use service providers such as Supabase, Vercel, and blockchain infrastructure, personal information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence. READS will take reasonable steps to ensure such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws.

8. Data Retention

8.1 Personal data is retained for as long as an account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed to resolve disputes, appeals, or legal obligations.

8.2 Details collected to support the review of a flagged exam (e.g. camera captures, incident logs) are deleted 14 days after READS reaches a decision, provided that decision is not appealed. Where a decision is appealed, those details are kept until the appeal is resolved, and deleted 14 days after that resolution.

9. Children's Privacy

9.1 Where a Learner is a minor, READS requires parent/guardian consent before the account is fully activated.

9.2 Front-camera monitoring during CBT exams applies equally to minors and is used solely for academic integrity purposes, never for any other form of monitoring.

9.3 A parent or guardian who provided consent for a minor's account may request access to, correction of, or deletion of that minor's personal information, where permitted by applicable law, by contacting us using the details in Section 13.

10. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No system can be guaranteed completely secure; account and wallet security responsibilities are set out in the Terms & Conditions.

11. Data Breach Notification

If READS becomes aware of a personal data breach likely to affect users, we will notify affected users and the relevant regulators, where required by applicable law.

12. Your Rights

Subject to applicable data protection law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and may object to or request restriction of certain processing, by contacting us using the details in Section 13. Requests may be declined where READS is legally required or otherwise permitted to retain certain information, including for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or academic integrity investigations.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the platform, and continued use of the platform after such notice constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns, contact us through:

  • The in-app support/dispute portal (primary channel), or
  • Email: readstechnologies@gmail.com
