These Learner Terms & Conditions govern the use of the $READS platform by Learners. Tutors, Tutorial Centres, Schools, and Examination Centres should instead refer to the Partner Terms & Conditions. Learners taking part in the Smart User Challenge should also read the Tournament Terms & Rules.

1. Definitions

1.1 READS — The READS team, being the operator of the platform and the party responsible for verification, review, and enforcement of these terms.

1.2 Learner — The normal user of the READS platform, who books sessions with Tutors or Tutorial Centres, and/or registers for exams through an Examination Centre.

1.3 Tutor — A verified teacher who is paid to conduct one-on-one (1v1) learning sessions with Learners online.

1.4 Tutorial Centre — Functionally similar to a Tutor, except tutors operate as a group under a single umbrella body (the "Centre"), which manages session requests and payouts on their behalf.

1.5 School — A verified physical or online school registered as a partner on the platform.

1.6 Examination Centre — A physical registration centre responsible for the physical registration of exams on behalf of Learners.

2. Eligibility

2.1 Anyone may sign up as a Learner, subject to clause 2.2.

2.2 Learners who are minors must have the consent of a parent or guardian to use the platform. READS may require this consent to be recorded or verified before the minor's account is fully activated.

3. Payments

3.1 All Learner payments — for sessions, exam registration, and marketplace purchases — are made in $READS tokens only.

3.2 A service fee of 5% applies to all such payment transactions, and is paid by the Learner (as the sender). This excludes network transaction fees.

4. Booking & Completing Sessions

4.1 A Learner may request a session with a Tutor, or with a Tutorial Centre (which will route the request to an available tutor under its umbrella).

4.2 A session is verified through the completion process below: either the Learner marking it complete, or the Learner accepting a Tutor-marked completion. This confirmation is itself the verification step — no separate READS check is required before payout, except where a dispute is raised.

4.3 Marking a Session Complete

  • If the Learner marks a session as completed, payout to the Tutor (or Tutorial Centre) is released immediately.
  • If the Tutor (or Tutorial Centre) marks a session as complete, the Learner will be asked to accept or reject that completion:
    • If the Learner accepts, payment is released as normal.
    • If the Learner rejects, the session is submitted to READS as a dispute.

5. Disputes, Refunds & Cancellations

5.1 Disputed or cancelled sessions are reviewed by READS on a case-by-case basis.

5.2 Where a session is found to be partially completed, the payment is split: half is released to the Tutor (or Tutorial Centre), and half is refunded to the Learner.

5.3 Where a session did not happen at all, the Learner receives a full refund.

5.4 If a party believes there was an error in a dispute decision, that decision may be appealed in line with the Appeals Procedure in Section 14.

5.5 Approved refunds are processed immediately, subject to applicable blockchain confirmation times.

6. Exam Registration

6.1 All exam registrations submitted through an Examination Centre are marked pending until the Centre accepts them.

6.2 Once accepted, the Centre will notify the Learner of when to appear for physical registration, and the registration moves to a "Pending Centre Processing" status.

6.3 The registration remains in "Pending Centre Processing" until the Centre completes it and submits proof to READS for review. It is only marked complete once READS approves that proof.

7. Academic Integrity (CBT Exams)

7.1 Monitoring — During a CBT exam, the platform monitors for the following:

  • Copying question text or right-clicking on the exam screen
  • Switching away from the exam tab/app
  • Answering a question implausibly fast
  • Taking a screenshot
  • Using the front camera to detect a second device being used to photograph the screen

7.2 Warnings vs. Instant Flags

  • Tab-switching and implausibly fast answers are treated as possible accidents: the Learner receives an on-screen warning for the 1st and 2nd occurrence. A 3rd or further occurrence flags the exam.
  • Copy/screenshot attempts and front-camera irregularities (e.g. face repeatedly out of frame) are flagged immediately, with no warning, as these require deliberate action.

7.3 Timing — Each question has a countdown timer. When it expires, the question is auto-submitted (with or without an answer) and the exam moves to the next question.

7.4 Watermarking — Every question screen displays a visible watermark containing the Learner's ID, a timestamp, and the session ID, so that any leaked screenshot or photo can be traced back to its source.

7.5 No Interruption — A flag does not pause or interrupt the exam. The Learner is only informed that their exam was flagged after they complete it.

7.6 Review, Not Automatic Disqualification — Flagged exams are held for human review rather than being automatically disqualified. If the review clears the exam, rewards and results are released normally.

7.7 Appeals — Any review decision on a flagged exam may be appealed, in line with the Appeals Procedure in Section 14.
