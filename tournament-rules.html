Smart User Challenge — Tournament Terms & Rules

1. Overview

The Smart User Challenge is READS' annual points-based quiz tournament. Throughout the tournament period, qualified Learners are quizzed randomly based on what they have studied over the year. These Tournament Terms & Rules supplement, and should be read alongside, the READS Learner Terms & Conditions.

2. Definitions

2.1 School User — A Learner who has joined a School on the platform.

2.2 Non-School User — A Learner who has not joined a School on the platform.

2.3 Qualified Learner — A Learner selected to take part in the tournament, having received an access code.

2.4 Fair Points — A secondary score awarded per correct answer (plus a flat award for the daily bonus quiz, per Section 6), used for ranking and tie-breaking alongside the main point score.

2.5 Stage — One of the sequential tournament rounds: Regional, National, or Global.

3. Eligibility & Qualification

3.1 School Users are ranked on their school's leaderboard. Non-School Users are ranked on a regional leaderboard.

3.2 At the start of the tournament, the top ten (10) users from every school's leaderboard, and the top ten (10) users from the non-school regional leaderboard, are selected and sent access codes to unlock the tournament.

3.3 READS may require a minimum level of activity (for example, a minimum number of completed lessons or quizzes) before a Learner is eligible for qualification, to prevent inactive accounts from qualifying simply because a leaderboard has few participants.

3.4 An access code must be entered to participate. Access codes are personal to the Qualified Learner they are issued to and may not be shared or transferred.

4. Age Divisions

The tournament runs across four divisions:

  • U-15 Smart User Challenge
  • U-18 Smart User Challenge
  • U-21 Smart User Challenge
  • The Smart User Challenge (Open category)

5. Format & Duration

5.1 The tournament runs across several Stages, each with its own duration and quiz count — see Section 9 (Advancement & Schedule) for the full timeline.

5.2 In the Qualification round, each Qualified Learner receives twenty (20) random quizzes, drawn from what they have studied on the platform. Quiz counts for later Stages are set out in Section 9.

5.3 Quizzes are released weekly: five (5) quizzes per week at the regular pace. A Qualified Learner who completes their weekly quizzes faster is given early access to up to two (2) quizzes from the following week, for a maximum of seven (7) quizzes in that week. At the end of each week, revision notes are provided to help Qualified Learners prepare for the following week's quizzes.

5.4 A quiz not completed within its release week carries over into the following week's allowance rather than expiring.

5.5 A daily bonus quiz of three (3) questions is also available to Qualified Learners throughout the tournament period.

5.6 Quizzes are randomly generated from topics each Qualified Learner has already studied on the platform, and are balanced according to READS' standard assessment criteria. Because quizzes are drawn from each Learner's own study history, question difficulty may vary between participants; this variation is expected and is not, by itself, a basis for dispute.

6. Scoring

6.1 Main Quizzes — Each quiz contains ten (10) questions. Points are awarded based on accuracy:

  • 90% or higher: 3 points
  • 70%–89%: 2 points
  • 50%–69%: 1 point

6.2 Fair Points (Main Quizzes) — Fair Points are awarded purely based on correct answers: each correctly answered question earns 1 Fair Point, for up to 10 Fair Points on a perfect quiz. No additional Fair Points are awarded simply for completing a quiz.

6.3 Daily Bonus Quiz — Passing the 3-question daily bonus quiz with 70% or higher accuracy earns 10 Fair Points.

7. Tie-Breakers

Where two or more users are tied on points, ranking is decided in this order:

  • Higher Fair Points total
  • If still tied, faster average completion time across quizzes
  • If participants remain tied after both tie-breakers above, READS may issue an additional tie-break quiz or declare a shared ranking, depending on the Stage.

8. Anti-Cheat & Integrity

8.1 If cheating is suspected on a quiz submission, that submission is flagged and held for human review, in line with the CBT anti-cheat mechanisms described in the Learner Terms & Conditions.

8.2 Where a Qualified Learner is found guilty on review, 5 points and 15 Fair Points are deducted from their tournament score.

8.3 A Qualified Learner found guilty three (3) times over the course of the tournament is automatically disqualified. This count is cumulative across the entire tournament and does not reset between Stages.

8.4 Effects of Disqualification — A disqualified Qualified Learner forfeits all points and Fair Points accumulated in the tournament, and is removed from further advancement. As a disqualified Learner has not yet been ranked as a winner, no prize forfeiture arises from disqualification itself; see Section 10.2 for prize handling where a winner is later found to have cheated.

8.5 A guilty verdict may be appealed. Given the tournament's time-boxed nature, tournament appeals follow a faster timeline than the standard appeals procedure: the Qualified Learner has 3 days to file an appeal, and READS will respond within 2 days. The decision on appeal is final.

9. Advancement & Schedule

9.1 The tournament begins with the top ten (10) School Users from each school and the top ten (10) Non-School Users from each region being called up to compete in the Qualification round.

9.2 The top three (3) users from each qualifying group advance to the Regional Tournament. At the Regional Tournament, the same rules apply; the top three (3) advance to the National Tournament. The National finalists advance to the Global Tournament, which is held once during each tournament year.

Prizes are distributed in $READS tokens, subject to the standard payout terms in the Learner Terms & Conditions.

10.1 To receive a prize, a winner must be reachable through their registered contact details and must provide a valid wallet address.

10.2 Where a winner cannot be reached, provides invalid wallet information, or is found — even after results are announced — to have cheated, that winner forfeits their prize, and the prize passes to the next-ranked participant in that Stage.

11. Certificates

Winners at every Stage (Regional, National, and Global, where applicable) are awarded certificates. Every Qualified Learner who takes part in the tournament also receives a participation certificate, regardless of how far they advance.
